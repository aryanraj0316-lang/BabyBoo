# Google Play Console Content Rating (IARC) Questionnaire Guide

**Application:** BabyBoo (`com.babyboo.app`)  
**Target Category:** Utility / Tools / Parental Control  
**Expected Rating:** Everyone (ESRB) / PEGI 3 / USK 0 / ClassInd Livre / ACB G

---

## 1. General Category Selection
- **App Category:** Select **"Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other"** (do NOT select "Game" or "Social Network").

---

## 2. IARC Questionnaire Questions & Exact Answers

| Questionnaire Section | Question | Answer | Details / Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Violence** | Does the app contain any violent material or depictions of violence? | **No** | BabyBoo contains no violent themes, weapons, blood, or aggressive imagery. |
| **Fear / Horror** | Does the app contain scary or horrifying depictions? | **No** | The app simulates screen blackout, low battery, and system shutdown for screen-time management with friendly mascot guidance. It contains no horror themes. |
| **Sexuality** | Does the app contain sexual content or nudity? | **No** | Zero sexual themes, suggestive imagery, or nudity. |
| **Language** | Does the app contain offensive language or profanity? | **No** | All app text, dialogue, and mascot copy are clean, family-friendly, and polite. |
| **Controlled Substances** | Does the app contain references to drugs, alcohol, or tobacco? | **No** | Zero reference to controlled substances. |
| **Gambling / Monetization** | Does the app offer gambling, simulated gambling, or loot boxes? | **No** | No gambling, betting, simulated casino elements, or loot boxes. |
| **User Interaction** | Does the app natively allow users to interact or exchange content with other users through voice, text, or photo sharing? | **No** | There are no chat rooms, forums, direct messaging, or social networking features. |
| **Location Sharing** | Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with other users? | **No** | No location access requested or shared. |
| **Digital Purchases** | Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods with real currency? | **No** (or **Yes** only if Google Play Billing is added in the future; currently Free / No IAP). | No real-money transaction or in-app purchase flow in current release. |
| **Web Browser** | Does the app contain an unrestricted web browser? | **No** | The only external web link is an intent to open the Privacy Policy in the user's default browser. |
