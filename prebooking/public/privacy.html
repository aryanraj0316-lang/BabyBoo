This notice covers the BabyBoo pre-launch reservation page, not the application. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What you’re reserving

A place on our Android launch notification list. This is free, takes no payment, and does not guarantee a release date, purchase, app-store reservation or priority access.

What we save

Your email address, the time you consented, your active/unsubscribed status, and campaign tags in your link (for example, which Instagram advertisement brought you here). We do not request your child’s name, age or other child information. This form is for adults.

Why we save it

To maintain the launch list, understand which campaigns bring signups, and contact you about BabyBoo’s launch with your permission. We do not sell this list. The BabyBoo site owner can access the list through a password-protected dashboard. No advertising pixels are installed on this page.

Hosting and security

The page is hosted on Render. Reservation records are stored in the PostgreSQL database connected by the site owner, which may use a separate hosting provider. Hosting providers may keep operational logs, including connection information. The page briefly uses a hashed connection identifier to limit repeated requests; it is not added to your reservation record.

Creator referrals and early-access reviews

If you use a creator code, we save that code and its promised extra daily shutdown allowance with your reservation. This records a future benefit; the website does not change the limits in an installed app. Creators submit reviews through private invitations linked to their Instagram handle. We store the handle, rating, review, consent timestamp, trial/partnership disclosure, and moderation status. Only approved reviews are public. Trial access and referral partnerships are disclosed alongside published reviews. The invitation link is private; do not share it publicly. To request removal, contact BabyBoo through the same Instagram conversation used for your invitation; the owner can hide your review.

Leave the list

You can remove your email from the active launch list using this form. It gives the same response whether the address was listed or not. Any future launch email must include a way to unsubscribe. The email address remains stored as an unsubscribed entry to prevent accidental reactivation without new consent. The owner should remove launch-list records after the launch campaign is finished.
