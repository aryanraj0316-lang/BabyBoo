## Story context

BabyBoo helps a parent end a child's phone session gently. Buddy is a warm companion, not a disciplinarian. The phone plays five reels, warns of a low battery, drains from 5% to 0%, switches off, rests, and allows a parent to unlock it by drawing a freehand A on its dark screen. Buddy watches, becomes mildly concerned, accepts that time is up, gets sleepy, notices the parent, points toward the unlock action, then celebrates and reassures the family.

Concern must be mild and readable, never frightening, tearful, panicked or angry. The sleepy section is a deliberate peaceful pause. The ending is delighted but gentle.
